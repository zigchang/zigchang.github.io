    <p>I am an AI Algorithm Expert at Alibaba Taotian Group, where I work on multimodal foundation models, intelligent agents, and generative vision. My interests lie in building AI systems that are not only capable, but also reliable, controllable, and useful in real products.</p>

    <p>My recent work spans large-model post-training, quality evaluation, product understanding, text-to-video generation, digital humans, and document intelligence. Before joining Alibaba, I was an AI Research Scientist at Baidu and Duxiaoman Finance. I received my Ph.D. in Information and Communication Engineering from Shanghai Jiao Tong University.</p>

    <h2>Research interests</h2>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Multimodal foundation models:</strong> post-training, distillation, alignment, and data quality for vision-language models.</li>
      <li><strong>Intelligent agents:</strong> evaluation and retrieval-augmented workflows for reliable long-context and knowledge-intensive tasks.</li>
      <li><strong>Generative vision:</strong> controllable video generation, digital-human animation, and visual content understanding.</li>
      <li><strong>Trustworthy AI systems:</strong> scalable evaluation, error analysis, and quality governance for production models.</li>
    </ul>
